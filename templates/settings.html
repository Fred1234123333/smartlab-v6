Danger Zone

These actions permanently delete data and cannot be undone. Type DELETE exactly to confirm each one.

Delete All Reports

Removes every report (open and resolved). Computers under repair only because of a report will return to Working.

Reset Computer Records

Deletes every computer and all of its reports, and removes their QR code image files.

Reset Application Data

Deletes all computers and reports and clears all QR code files. Your admin account and saved settings are kept, so you will not be locked out.
